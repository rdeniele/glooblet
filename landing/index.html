Learn

A few things worth actually knowing

Mouse settings get a lot of marketing noise. Here's what each term really changes, in plain terms, whether or not you ever install Glooblet.

Higher DPI isn't "better"

DPI controls how far your cursor moves on screen per inch your hand moves the mouse. A higher number means more on-screen travel for the same physical motion, not more precision. A modern sensor tracks just as accurately at 800 DPI as it does at 8000. Most competitive players sit between 400 and 1600 and shape the actual feel with in-game sensitivity instead.

Polling rate is about delay, not accuracy

Your mouse reports its position to Windows a fixed number of times per second (its polling rate, usually 125–1000 Hz). A higher rate means the system hears about a movement sooner, shaving a few milliseconds of input lag. It doesn't make the sensor more accurate or tracking smoother at ordinary speeds.

"Enhance pointer precision" is acceleration

Despite the name, this Windows setting adds acceleration: the cursor travels further when you move the mouse quickly than when you move it slowly, for the same physical distance. That's handy for desktop work, but it makes muscle memory unreliable in games, which is why most players turn it off.

cm/360° is the number that actually travels

The same DPI and sensitivity mean different things in different games, because each engine applies its own multiplier between a raw mouse count and a degree of camera turn. cm/360° (how far your mouse has to travel across the pad for a full turn) is the portable unit. Convert to it, and your aim feels the same wherever you're playing.

Built to stay out of your games

Glooblet never reads or writes game memory, never injects code, and never automates gameplay. Everything it does is visible, user-mode, and built from documented Windows APIs. See the full policy in docs/SECURITY.md.

"Glooblet is designed to configure supported mouse and desktop settings without automating gameplay. Game compatibility depends on each game's policies, anti-cheat system, hardware, and the specific integration used. Glooblet does not guarantee that every third-party integration is permitted by every game."
